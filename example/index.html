App Mode Controls

How it works

  • Click "Enter App Mode" to transform the form into a fullscreen experience
  • The form becomes the focus, eliminating distractions
  • Body scrolling is prevented to avoid layout issues
  • Click "Exit App Mode" to return to normal layout
  • Use "Toggle" to switch between modes
